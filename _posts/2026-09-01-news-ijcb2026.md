I'm excited to announce that our paper **"A Valid Watermark Is Not a Valid Identity: Spatial-Spectral Adversarial Face Evasion with Watermark Preservation"** has been accepted at the [IEEE International Joint Conference on Biometrics (IJCB 2026)](https://ijcb2026.ieee-biometrics.org/), in the special session *When Biometrics Break (WBB): Security Implications of Performance Degradation, Misclassification, and Operational Drift*.

## What the paper is about

Biometric documents require two independent guarantees: **provenance** (source, issuer, integrity) and **biometric reliability** (identity match, authentication). The usual intuition assumes that a valid watermark plus a trusted face image implies both. We challenge this assumption by introducing a watermark-preserving adversarial face evasion attack.

Our proposed PGD-L2 attack uses spatial and spectral preservation terms to reduce face-recognition similarity while limiting distortion to the embedded watermark — all under a **black-box watermarking setting**, without access to the watermark decoder during optimization. The attack achieves:

- **Evasion rates above 92%** at operational thresholds (FAR=0.1%)
- **Watermark recovery above 93%** for StegaFormer and above **99.7%** for FaceSigns
- **Visual quality above 47 dB PSNR** at ε₂ = 2.0

These results expose a **structural gap** in watermark-based facial certification: a face image can remain watermark-valid and visually faithful while becoming biometrically unlinked.
